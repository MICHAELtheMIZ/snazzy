Privacy Policy

How Snazzy Paws Grooming collects, uses and protects customer information.

Privacy Statement

Snazzy Paws Grooming collects only the information needed to manage bookings, answer enquiries and provide grooming services.

What We Collect

We may collect customer names, contact details and records of interactions with the salon, such as booking requests and enquiry messages.

Why We Collect It

This information helps staff respond to customers, confirm appointment details and provide appropriate grooming services.

Optional Information

Some information is optional, but contact details are needed if customers want Snazzy Paws Grooming to process a booking or respond to a message.

Official Privacy Policy

We collect personal information from you, including information about your:

  • name
  • contact information
  • interactions with us

We collect your personal information in order to:

  • manage customer bookings and respond to enquiries

Providing some information is optional. If you choose not to enter contact information, we'll be unable to process bookings or respond to enquiries.

You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong. If you’d like to ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected, please contact us at contact@snazzypawsgrooming.co.nz, or 07 123 4567.

Contact About Privacy

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact form or discussed directly with the grooming salon.
