Privacy Policy

How Snazzy Paws Grooming collects, uses and protects customer information.

Privacy Statement

Snazzy Paws Grooming collects personal information only when it is needed to respond to enquiries, manage booking requests and provide grooming services.

Information We Collect

Information may include a customer's name, email address, phone number, dog's name, breed, age and selected grooming service.

How We Use Information

Customer information is used to confirm appointments, prepare for grooming services, respond to messages and improve the website experience.

Storage and Security

Information submitted through the website should be stored securely, accessed only by authorised staff and kept only for as long as it is needed for business purposes.

Sharing Information

Snazzy Paws Grooming does not sell customer information. Information is shared only when required to provide a requested service or meet a legal obligation.

Customer Rights

Customers can contact Snazzy Paws Grooming to ask what personal information is held about them, request corrections or ask for information to be removed where appropriate.

Contact About Privacy

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact form or discussed directly with the grooming salon.
